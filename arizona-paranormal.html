Haunted Echoes Studios · An Original Fictional World

The 9th Vessel

A 1–4 player cooperative paranormal investigation and survival-horror mystery set in Calder, Arizona, a wholly fictional copper-mining town.

On Halloween weekend, Calder's Main Street festival attracts visitors expecting an ordinary ghost hunt. As the team explores, shifting clues and increasingly unnatural encounters suggest the town has a history nobody wants to confront.

Welcome to

CALDER, ARIZONA

A historic copper town on an Arizona plateau overlooking a desert basin. Festival lights fill the present-day streets; older structures and forgotten records invite a different kind of exploration.

Original Calder environment illustrations and gameplay footage are in development. Earlier venue-based concept imagery has been retired from public display.

Story Foundation

The mystery has a fixed truth.

Evidence placement, paranormal events and AI-directed pressure can differ between investigations. The hidden history stays consistent, but what the team discovers and the choices it makes can change its experience.

Investigate

Search original locations, recover evidence and work together during an increasingly dangerous ghost hunt.

Interpret

Connect records, witness accounts, environmental clues and encounters to uncover the town's hidden story.

Choose

The team's discoveries and decisions affect its path through the mystery.

World Building

Historically inspired. Entirely fictional.

Real Arizona mining towns are research references only. Calder has its own history, streets, commercial buildings and explorable interiors, designed without requiring a one-for-one recreation of real properties.
